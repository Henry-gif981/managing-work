**14. How the IBRD differs** — Two-column comparison · ground: wash · ref: 1.1.2
<br>2D: map · 3D: — (2D only)

- [left] IBRD
  - Oldest and largest: founded in 1944; the largest development bank in the world
  - Pricing: market-based interest rates
  - Clients: governments, or entities guaranteed by governments
- [right] IDA · IFC · MIGA · ICSID
  - IDA: much cheaper terms, funded by donors, for the poorest countries
  - IFC: invests in private companies without a government guarantee
  - MIGA and ICSID: no direct finance; reduce investor risk, settle disputes
